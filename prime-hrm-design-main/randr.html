Rewards & Recognition (R & R)

Elements System Indicators Evidence Requirements
Policy The Agency’s R&R policies and processes are proactively reviewed and updated to respond to changes in the Agency's strategic goal.
Structures and Roles The Agency has an R&R Committee that collaborates with top management, managers/supervisors and HRMO in proactively reviewing and updating R&R system to respond to changes in the Agency's strategic goals.
Review Mechanism The Agency uses R&R-generated data to evaluate R&R function, and make strategic decisions to ensure continuing alignment and responsiveness of R&R to its strategic goals.
Information and Communication (use of technology) The agency has an Enterprise Information Management System that links the core HRM areas with other HRM and organizational systems (e.g. benefits, loans, payroll, etc.).
Information and Communication (database content) The Agency maintains documentation and records on:
  • Historical data on efficiency and effectiveness of R&R system.
  • R&R policy and process flow to other HRM and organizational systems
Elements System Indicators Evidence Requirements
Planning
  • The Agency's R&R plans and programs are directly linked to Agency's strategic long-term (over 3 years) plan.
  • The Agency has a process for reviewing R&R programs to ensure that these continually support achievement of strategic goals and performance objectives, and meet employees' recognition and motivational needs.
Elements System Indicators Evidence Requirements
R&R Screening and Selection Criteria and Processes The Agency's R&R screening and selection criteria and processes are regularly reviewed and improved to ensure consistency, fairness and objectivity in selection decisions.
